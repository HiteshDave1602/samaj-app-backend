Pragati Colour & Type

Colour scheme

Seven hues taken from the logo, turned into accessible roles for light and dark mode. Use the role tokens in your UI, not the raw reference values. Every text pairing below meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 or higher).

Typography

A rounded display face that matches the logo's Gujarati lettering, a clean sans for everything else, and a monospace for codes and numbers.
